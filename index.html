Net new dwelling units on building permits issued, by building type. Current year is year-to-date.

Each approved neighbourhood plan (NSP / NASP) sets a buildout target in its Land Use & Population Statistics table. This ledger sets those targets against the dwellings on the ground today, and compares the housing mix the plan assumed with the mix actually being permitted.

Redevelopment plans (ARPs)

ARPs have no standard statistics table, so each is measured against the target it states: total units, new units since adoption, or residents at buildout.
